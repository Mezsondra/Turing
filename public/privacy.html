Privacy Policy

Turing Test Challenge · last updated 29 August 2026

This game pairs you with a stranger or with an AI for about a minute, and you guess which one you were talking to. This page says exactly what that involves for your data. It is short because the game collects little.

What we store

DataWhy
A random device ID, generated in your browser So your score and streak survive a refresh without making you sign up. It identifies a browser, not a person.
Your score, streak, and one row per round (human or AI, what you guessed, whether you were right) Your stats and history.
A one-way hash of your IP address To count free rounds against the daily limit, and to enforce a suspension if your account is suspended for abuse. We do not store the address itself and cannot recover it from the hash.
If you create an account: your email address, a bcrypt hash of your password, and your chosen username To sign you in. We never store your password itself.
If you sign in with Google: your email address, and your name if Google supplies it To sign you in.
If you subscribe: your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your plan, its status and billing period To know whether you have Premium. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us.
Your language, sound and vibration settings Stored in your browser only. They never reach our server.

Chat messages

We do not keep a copy of your conversations. Messages are relayed between the two players in real time and are not written to our database. There are exactly two exceptions, and both exist for safety:

  • If someone reports a conversation, the transcript they submit is stored with the report so a human can review it.
  • Every message sent to a human partner is checked by an automated classifier. If it flags something, that single message is stored as a moderation record for a human to review.

When your opponent is the AI, your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate its replies. Google processes them under its own terms.

Who else sees your data

  • Google (Gemini API) - messages in AI matches, and messages checked by the moderation classifier.
  • Google (Sign-In) - only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Stripe - payment processing. Stripe collects your card details directly; we never receive them.
  • Resend - delivers your sign-in code email.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. There is currently no advertising network in the web version.

How long we keep it

Account and game data is kept until you delete your account. Moderation records are kept after that: an account deletion must not erase evidence of abuse against someone else.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete Account, in the app. It permanently removes your user record, game history, subscription record and blocks. Two things deliberately survive: reports filed against you, and the hashed IP - otherwise deleting an account would be a way to reset the daily round limit, or to lift a suspension.

Deleting your account does not cancel Stripe billing. Cancel first, via Settings → Manage Subscription.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, EU, or another region with data protection law, you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email the address below and we will respond within 30 days. Deletion is also available immediately in the app, above.

Children

This app is rated 17+ and is not for children. It puts you in unmoderated live chat with strangers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 17; if you believe a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Data lives on a single server in the United Kingdom.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the app will say so before you keep playing.

Contact

Privacy questions, data requests and abuse reports: support@turing-test.app. Abuse reports get a response within 24 hours.
