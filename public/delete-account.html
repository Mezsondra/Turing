Delete your account

Turing Test Challenge · turing-test.app

You can delete your account and its data at any time. There are two ways, and both reach the same place.

In the app

Open Settings → Delete Account. It takes effect immediately, and no email or waiting period is involved.

By email

If you have already uninstalled the app, write to support@turing-test.app from the email address on the account, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm within 24 hours and delete within 30 days.

If you played as a guest and never signed up, there is no account to find: your data was keyed to an identifier held in your browser, and clearing the app's data removes the link to it permanently.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password, username
  • Your score, streaks, and every round you have played
  • Your subscription record
  • Everyone you have blocked, and every report you filed

What is kept, and why

Two things deliberately survive deletion:

  • Reports filed against you by other players, if any. These are moderation records about someone else's experience, and deleting an account must not be a way to erase evidence of abuse.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address, which is used to count free rounds and to enforce suspensions. We cannot recover the address from it. Without this, deleting an account would be a way to reset the daily round limit or lift a suspension.

Neither can be used to identify you or to rebuild your account.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not stop billing. If you subscribed:

  • On the web — Settings → Manage Subscription, before deleting.
  • On iPhone or iPad — Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • On Android — Play Store → Payments and subscriptions.
